# Veyo Media: BOF creative briefs

Briefs for the 7 ads in [bof-messaging.md](bof-messaging.md). The ad copy (primary text, headline,
description, CTA) lives there; this file covers what goes in the image or video.

## Rules for every creative

- **Sizes:** make each one in 4:5 (1080×1350, feed) and 9:16 (1080×1920, Stories/Reels).
  In 9:16, keep text and faces out of the top 250px and bottom 340px (Meta's buttons cover them).
- **Colours:** ink `#1E1B5E` or paper `#FAFAF7` backgrounds. Lime `#C6F24E` only on the one thing
  the eye should hit first (a number, a tick, the logo dot). No gradients on ink.
- **Type:** Geist Bold for headlines, Geist Mono for numbers. The veyo! logo small, bottom corner.
- **Images:** real, anonymised screenshots only (blur brand names, product photos and account IDs).
  No stock photos, no handshakes, no rockets. No Meta or Facebook logos.
- **Text on image:** 7 words or fewer for the main line. The ad copy does the explaining.
- **Video:** burned-in captions (most people watch muted), hook in the first 2 seconds, 15–30 seconds,
  filmed on a phone in natural light. You on camera beats any animation.
- **Numbers:** any ROAS figure on an image carries "Past results don't guarantee future performance."
  Mock-up numbers must be labelled "Example".

## New · HV

### HV1: P&L, not platform · static (+ optional 15s video)

**Idea:** two numbers that disagree. Ads Manager's ROAS vs what the store actually made.

**Layout (4:5):**
- Paper background, split into two cards side by side.
- Left card, slate grey: small label "Ads Manager says", big Geist Mono number "4.0x".
- Right card, ink: small label "Your P&L says", big number "1.6x" with a lime underline.
- Main line above: **"Which one pays your rent?"**
- Bottom: small "Example" tag, logo bottom right.

**9:16:** stack the cards vertically, line on top.

**Optional video (15s, you on camera):**
- 0–2s: hold your phone up to the camera showing an Ads Manager screen (anonymised). "This says 4x."
- 2–5s: switch to a Shopify screen. "This says otherwise."
- 5–12s: "We report Meta ads against your store data, MER and new-customer cost, not the platform number."
- 12–15s: "Free audit, link below."

**Assets needed:** one anonymised Ads Manager screenshot and the matching Shopify/P&L view (only for the video version).

### HV2: The lock-in breakup · static carousel (3 cards) or single static

**Idea:** the comparison table from the site, turned into a breakup.

**Single static (4:5):**
- Ink background. Main line in white: **"It's not you. It's your contract."**
- Below, three rows, each with a slate strikethrough on the left and a lime tick on the right:
  - ~~6–12 month lock-in~~ → Month-to-month
  - ~~Creative billed extra~~ → Creative included
  - ~~A junior on your account~~ → The specialist you meet
- Logo bottom right.

**Carousel version:** one row per card (same layout, larger), last card = "Your ad account stays yours." + logo.

**Assets needed:** none, it's type only.

### HV3: Proof · video (20–30s) + static backup

**Idea:** show the work, not just the number.

**Video (you on camera, screen recording cut in):**
- 0–2s: on-screen text **"8–10x average ROAS. Here's how."** You to camera: "2 years of Meta ads for Australian brands."
- 2–10s: screen recording, anonymised: a creative testing board or a campaign structure. "New ads every month, tested properly."
- 10–20s: screen recording of a report showing MER and new-customer cost. "Reported against the store, not the platform."
- 20–28s: you to camera: "Whether that's realistic for your account, I'll tell you in a free audit."
- Throughout, bottom of frame: "Past results don't guarantee future performance."

**Static backup (4:5):** ink background, "8–10x" huge in lime Geist Mono, under it "average return on ad spend, 2 years of Australian accounts", disclaimer line at the bottom in slate.

**Assets needed:** anonymised screen recordings; the evidence for 8–10x saved before this runs.

### HV4: Agencies (white label) · static

**Idea:** the agency's logo goes on the work; Veyo stays invisible.

**Layout (4:5):**
- Paper background. A simple mock report cover in the middle with a grey placeholder "YOUR AGENCY" logo.
- A small veyo! logo in the corner of the report, crossed out with a lime line.
- Main line: **"Our work. Your logo."**
- Bottom strip: "NDA · Non-solicitation · Partner access only"

**Assets needed:** none (build the report mock from a real Veyo report, with all data removed).

## New · LV

### LV1: Roast my ads (nicely) · video (20–30s), the hero LV ad

**Idea:** the site's "meh to veyo!" in motion. A bad ad, then the fixed version.

**Video (screen + voiceover or you on camera):**
- 0–2s: on-screen text **"Rating your ads. Nicely."** Show a made-up bad ad: stock-looking photo, "Shop now!!", no price.
- 2–10s: point at the problems with red circles: "No price. No reason to buy today. Button goes to the homepage."
- 10–20s: swipe to the fixed version: product in use, price in the headline, clear offer. "Same product. Now it sells."
- 20–28s: you to camera: "Send me yours. Free roast, no sales pitch, and I'll tell you if you even need an agency."

**Rules:** the "bad ad" must be one you made up, never a real brand's ad. Poke fun at the ad, not the person.

**Static backup (4:5):** the bad ad and the fixed ad side by side, labels "meh" (strikethrough) and "veyo!".

**Assets needed:** one made-up bad ad and one fixed ad (build both in Canva or Figma).

### LV2: Price on the page · static

**Idea:** a price tag in an industry that hides its prices.

**Layout (4:5):**
- Ink background. Small line in slate: "Most agencies: 'Let's jump on a call.'"
- Big white Geist Mono: **"A$1,190/mo"**, with "Creative included" under it with a lime tick.
- Three small ticks below: "6 new ads a month · Pixel + Conversions API checked · Month-to-month"
- Logo bottom right.

**Assets needed:** none. Check the price still matches `/pricing` before every launch.

### LV3: Not ready for an agency · static or 3-card carousel

**Idea:** show the checklist result screen so people can see what they'll get.

**Single static (4:5):**
- Paper background. A real screenshot of the `/meta-ads-audit` result screen in a phone frame, showing the score and level (Leaky / Steady / Scaling).
- Main line: **"Is your ad account leaky?"**
- Under it: "Free 60-point checklist. No call needed."

**Carousel version:**
1. "Is your ad account leaky?" + phone mock of the checklist
2. "60 points. 7 sections." + the section list
3. "Leaky, Steady or Scaling?" + the result screen, with the CTA

**Assets needed:** screenshots from the live checklist (use test details, not a real person's).
